Privacy Policy — ProdAssist

App: ProdAssist (com.rozhaustech.prodassist)
Developer: Rozha's Technologies (Rozha Richardson)
Contact: rozharichardson@gmail.com
Last updated: November 2026

Overview

ProdAssist is a camera and production app. We collect only what is needed to run features you use. We do not sell your personal data.

Data the app uses on your device

  • Camera and microphone — to preview, record, and stream video and audio when you use those features.
  • Photos and videos — saved to storage or gallery when you record or export, according to your settings.
  • Bluetooth and local network — to connect to tripods, remotes, and the Windows companion on your Wi‑Fi (video/control stays on your network unless you stream to a service you choose).
  • USB — when you use HDMI or UVC/webcam-style output supported on your device.

Data sent off the device

  • RTMP or streaming — only when you enter a stream URL and start streaming (sent to that destination).
  • Google Play billing — subscription status is handled by Google Play; we receive purchase tokens to unlock Pro/Studio features.
  • Bug reports — only if you choose Send in the in-app report flow (may include logs you approve).

Windows companion

The optional PC companion receives video from your phone over your local network when you enable that feature. It does not upload your stream to our servers by default.

Children

ProdAssist is not designed for children. It uses the camera and microphone and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families program.

Retention and deletion

Recordings stay on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes app-private data. Contact us to ask about data tied to support email.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always at this URL.
